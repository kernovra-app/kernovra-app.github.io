A PRACTICAL FLOW

From plan to history.

A small set of steps keeps the next action visible without turning release work into another dashboard.

01 / PREPARE

Check the essentials.

Create a release, choose a checklist, and document the preparation items that matter.

02 / RECORD

Capture the outcome.

Enter the result, dates, identifiers, notes, and local attachments yourself.

03 / REVIEW

Keep the context.

Return to the record, review what happened, and export a local summary when needed.

BUILT FOR CLARITY

Your records stay on your device until you choose to share them.

Kernovra does not connect a wallet, deploy contracts, or verify on-chain information. Results and identifiers are entered by you.
