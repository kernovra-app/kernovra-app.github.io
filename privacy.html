Privacy Policy

Kernovra is designed to keep your release work on your device.

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Information you enter

The app lets you create release names, project and network details, checklists, notes, results, addresses and transaction identifiers, and local attachments. This information is entered by you and stored locally on your device.

Data collection and sharing

Kernovra does not require an account and does not send your release records to a Kernovra server. The app does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. It does not connect a wallet, deploy contracts, or verify blockchain information.

If you export a CSV or PDF, share an attachment, or use the iOS share sheet, you choose the destination. The privacy practices of that destination then apply.

Attachments and notifications

Images and PDFs you import are copied into local app storage. If you allow notifications, the app may schedule reminders on your device. You can disable reminders in the app or change notification permission in iOS Settings.

Retention and deletion

Your records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the app. You can delete an individual release or use Workspace → Delete all local data. Exports and copies you saved elsewhere are not removed by deleting data in Kernovra.

This website

This support website does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or contact forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or website changes. The effective date above will be revised. For privacy questions, email kernovra.support@gmail.com.
