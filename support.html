COMMON QUESTIONS

Where are my release records stored?

Release records and local attachments are stored on your device. Kernovra does not sync them to an account or server.

Does Kernovra connect to a wallet or blockchain?

No. Kernovra is a manual release workspace. It does not connect a wallet, deploy contracts, or verify on-chain data.

How do I export a record?

Open Workspace → Export center to prepare a CSV summary or a single-release PDF. You choose where the exported file goes through the iOS share sheet.

Can I customize a checklist?

Open Workspace → Templates to create a checklist template or edit the built-in checklist. Changes to a template affect future releases; existing releases keep their copied checks.

How do I remove my data?

Open Workspace → Delete all local data. You can also delete an individual release from its edit screen. Deletion from the device cannot be undone unless you have separately saved a copy.

How do reminders work?

If you allow notifications, Kernovra can schedule local reminders for follow-up. You can turn reminders off in Workspace and manage notification permission in iOS Settings.
